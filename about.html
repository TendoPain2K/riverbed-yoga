Accessibility Accommodations

We welcome students with different abilities and experience levels. Chair-based options, yoga blocks, and extra rest breaks are available.

Tell us about your needs before class so we can discuss accommodations. You may also request a private session for individual guidance.

Studio Policies

  • Arrive 10 minutes early to settle in and speak with your instructor.
  • Silence your phone and respect other students' personal space.
  • Clean borrowed mats and return props after class.
  • Let us know at least 24 hours ahead if you need to cancel a session.
  • Take breaks whenever needed and stop any movement that causes pain.
