Our Yoga Services

Group Classes

Gentle Yoga

A slower class with simple stretches and breathing exercises. Suitable for beginners and students who prefer a relaxed pace.

Vinyasa Yoga

A flowing class that connects movement with breathing. Instructors provide options for students with different experience levels.

Restorative Yoga

A restful class using blankets, blocks, and supported poses. This class gives students time to slow down and relax.

Private Sessions

Private sessions provide individual guidance at your own pace. Ask about beginner instruction, pose modifications, or a flexible schedule.
