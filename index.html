Welcome to Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Mission

We offer a calm, welcoming space to move, breathe, and recharge. Our classes support beginners, busy professionals, and older adults.

New to Yoga?

You do not need experience or perfect flexibility to begin. Move at your own pace, take breaks, and ask your instructor for help.

Wear comfortable clothes and bring water. Mats and props are available.

Featured Class: Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga combines slow movements and relaxed breathing. Instructors offer options for different comfort levels.
